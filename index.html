HVAC Diagram / HVAC 系統圖

直接在下方畫布拖拉元件、連接 Chilled Water Loop（冰水迴路）/ Cooling Water Loop（冷卻水迴路）管線建立架構圖；若已有既有的架構圖 JSON 檔案，也可以用畫布工具列的「Import JSON」按鈕或直接拖曳檔案到畫布上載入，繼續編輯。完成後點擊「套用架構圖到 Cooling Units」，系統會自動解析並將灰區設備 (Chiller, Pump, Cooling Tower 等) 連動同步至 Cooling Units List。
Drag components directly onto the canvas below and connect the Chilled Water Loop / Cooling Water Loop piping to build the diagram. If you already have an existing diagram JSON file, you can also use the "Import JSON" button on the canvas toolbar, or drag and drop the file onto the canvas to load it and continue editing. When finished, click "Apply Diagram to Cooling Units" — the system will automatically parse the diagram and sync grey-space equipment (Chiller, Pump, Cooling Tower, etc.) to the Cooling Units List.

在畫布上完成繪製或載入後，需點擊此按鈕才會將架構圖同步進本頁面；點擊前的畫布內容不會自動儲存。
After you finish drawing or loading on the canvas, click this button to sync the diagram into this page; canvas content is not saved automatically before you click it.
